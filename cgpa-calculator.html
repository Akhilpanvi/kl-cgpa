CGPA Formula Used

CGPA = Σ(Grade Point × Credits) / Σ(Credits)

Only courses where Credits > 0 and the grade point is numeric (not GP or P with 0 credits) are included in the calculation. Courses with GP grade (certifications like AWS, Salesforce) and pass/fail courses with 0 credits are excluded from CGPA computation — they still show in the table.
