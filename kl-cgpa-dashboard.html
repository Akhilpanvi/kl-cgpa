⚡ How to sync your grades from ERP

▼

Drag this button to your browser's bookmarks bar.

📊 KL Sync Grades
Drag the gold "📊 KL Sync Grades" button above to your bookmarks bar (one-time setup).
Log in to newerp.kluniversity.in with your credentials and solve the captcha.
Go to Exam Section → End Exam Result (or click this link) so the grade table is visible.
Click the "📊 KL Sync Grades" bookmark. A success message will appear on the ERP page.
Switch back to this tab and click "↺ Check for sync" — your dashboard will update instantly.
